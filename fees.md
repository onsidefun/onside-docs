# Where every fee goes

## The routing table

Every flow in the system and its split. **Live** means it is running at kickoff; **later** means the rule is fixed but the flow opens during the tournament. One status note: the splits below are near-final. They are locked in intent, but a number can still be tuned before its flow opens, and any change lands in this table first.

| Flow | Detail | Split | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ticket entry** | $10 per ticket, USDC or SOL | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Live |
| **Pack purchase** | Paid in $ONSIDE, once the store opens | 70% jackpot · 30% burned | Later |
| **Creator fees** | Every $ONSIDE trade on pump.fun, once the token is live | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Later |
| **Ticket resale** | Marketplace sale price | 95% seller · 5% fee | Later |
| **Marketplace fee** | The 5% from each resale | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Later |
| **Merge** | Taping two tickets into one | terms to be announced | In development |
| **Revive** | A card pulled from Silver/Gold packs, no separate fee | priced inside the pack | Later |
| **Final table payout** | The whole pool, at the final whistle | 50 / 18 / 10 / 6 / 4 / 4 / 4 / 4 across the 8 seats | At the whistle |

## What does not take a cut

Nothing else. There is no team allocation skimming the pot, no hidden spread on payments, and no fee that is not in the table above. The 10% ops slices are the entire operating take of the game.
