# Where every fee goes

## The routing table

Every flow in the system and its exact split. **Live** means it is running at kickoff; **later** means the rule is fixed but the flow opens during the tournament.

| Flow | Detail | Split | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ticket entry** | $10 per ticket, USDC or SOL | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Live |
| **Pack purchase** | Paid in $ONSIDE | 70% jackpot · 30% burned | Live |
| **Creator fees** | Every $ONSIDE trade on pump.fun | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Live |
| **Ticket resale** | Marketplace sale price | 95% seller · 5% fee | Later |
| **Marketplace fee** | The 5% from each resale | 90% jackpot · 10% ops | Later |
| **Revive fee** | Buying a dead ticket back over the line | 70% jackpot · 30% burned | Later |
| **Eliminated Boost stake** | $ONSIDE riding a ticket that gets cut | 30% jackpot · 70% burned | Later |

## What does not take a cut

Nothing else. There is no team allocation skimming the pot, no hidden spread on payments, and no fee that is not in the table above. The 10% ops slices are the entire operating take of the game.
