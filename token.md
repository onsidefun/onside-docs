# $ONSIDE

## What it does

$ONSIDE is the home token. It is not live yet: it launches on pump.fun later in the cup, and holders get perks once it does. Inside the game it is the **only** way to buy packs once the pack store opens, and the revive economy runs through it once revives open. It is never required to enter the game itself; tickets are USDC and SOL.

## The flywheel

The token sits inside the game's loop in three ways:

* **Demand.** Packs and revives are paid in $ONSIDE, so playing the in-game economy means buying the token.
* **Burn.** 30% of every pack and 30% of every revive are burned. Supply only shrinks.
* **Creator fees.** Every $ONSIDE trade on pump.fun generates creator fees, and 90% of those route into the jackpot. Trading volume literally grows the prize.

Put together: a bigger pot pulls in more players, more players rip more packs and trade more tickets, every one of those fees lands back in the pot while the burn shrinks supply, and the bigger pot pulls in more players. Round and round.

## What it is not

{% hint style="warning" %}
The flywheel is a routing design, not a promise of price. $ONSIDE has a job inside this game; whether anyone values that is the market's call, and nothing in these docs says otherwise.
{% endhint %}
