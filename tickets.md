# Tickets

## Price and cap

A ticket costs **$10**, paid in USDC or SOL, and a wallet can hold at most **10 tickets**. No whales: the cap means nobody can buy their way into dominating the board, and a bigger bag buys more runs, not better odds.

Each ticket plays independently. Ten tickets means ten sets of calls, ten board positions, and ten chances to be the last one standing.

## Where the $10 goes

* **$9 (90%)** goes straight into the jackpot. It is the pot, not revenue.
* **$1 (10%)** goes to ops and keeps the lights on.

{% hint style="info" %}
**No house edge in disguise:** the 90/10 split is the entire take. There is no spread on the payment, no slippage capture, and no fee on top.
{% endhint %}

## Your ticket is an NFT

Every ticket is a Metaplex Core NFT minted to your own wallet, not a row in our database wearing a costume. The ticket carries its board rank and points, and whoever holds it holds the position: you can keep it, gift it, or sell it on the marketplace once resale opens. A ticket order only activates after its payment receipt is verified on Solana.
